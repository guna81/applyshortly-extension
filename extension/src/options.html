Apply Shortly Form Assistant

Fills job applications from your ApplyShortly profile. You review and click Submit, or choose Auto apply to submit a complete form for you.

Checking connection…

1Connect your account

Advanced: manual API-key connection

For manual connection, create a per-user key in ApplyShortly → Settings → API keys.

2Apply to a job

  1. Start it. In ApplyShortly, choose Open & Autofill on a job. Or, on any application page, click the extension icon.
  2. Review. A panel at the bottom right lists what was filled and what still needs you. Click a row to jump to that field. Green means filled, amber means check it, red means it's your turn.
  3. Submit. Click the form's own Submit button. ApplyShortly moves the job to Submitted for you. Or use ⚡ Auto apply: once every required field is answered, it clicks Submit for you after a 3-second countdown you can cancel.
